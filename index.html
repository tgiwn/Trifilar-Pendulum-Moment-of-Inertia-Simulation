02 · APPARATUS GEOMETRY

Geometry and dimensional uncertainty

H is the vertical separation between the upper and lower attachment planes in the resting position. When upper and lower radii are equal, this reduces to the usual ideal small-angle relation with R².

03 · LOADED MEASUREMENT

Platform plus specimen

The mean period is calculated as T₁ = t₁ / N₁. The number of oscillations is treated as an exact count; the timing uncertainty should reflect your timer and measurement procedure.

04 · BASELINE

Empty platform measurement

The baseline run must use the same apparatus geometry and measurement configuration. The loaded mass includes the platform plus the specimen; the baseline mass is the empty platform assembly.

Idealized relationship

I = mgRrT² / (4π²H), with T = elapsed time / oscillation count.

With baseline subtraction: Ispecimen = Iloaded − Iplatform.

Uncertainties are first-order standard uncertainties. Mass and timing inputs are treated as independent, and the same geometry is treated as shared/correlated between loaded and baseline runs. The model treats H, R, and r uncertainties as mutually independent; geometry covariance and systematic model errors are not included.
